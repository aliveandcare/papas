Hunters for the Hungry – Tennessee

Paw Paw’s Place is honored to participate in the Tennessee Department of Agriculture’s Hunters for the Hungry–style program, allowing hunters to donate venison to feed families in need.

Turn Your Harvest Into a Blessing

If you’re a hunter, you can donate all or part of your deer to help provide high-quality protein to local families and ministries. It’s a simple way to give back using something you already love to do.

  1. Harvest your deer legally and tag it as required by Tennessee law.
  2. Bring your deer to Paw Paw’s Place and let us know you’d like to donate.
  3. We process the meat according to program guidelines and it is distributed to families and organizations in need.

Program details, coverage of processing costs, and limits can vary by season. Please contact us or check current Tennessee Department of Agriculture information for the latest guidelines.

Important Notes & Common Questions

Do you require a deposit?

Deposits may be required for beef and hog processing and during busy seasons. Please call for current deposit amounts and policies.

What about field dressing and cleanliness?

To protect all customers, we reserve the right to refuse any animal that is unsanitary, improperly dressed, or poses a food-safety concern. Clean, quickly chilled animals produce the best meat.

Do you process other wild game?

Our primary wild-game focus is deer. Other species may be considered on a case-by-case basis. Call us to ask about your specific needs.

Are you open year-round?

We operate year-round, but our schedule changes seasonally. During peak deer season, we may pause beef and pork to focus on deer. Call for current availability.

Is this a ministry?

Yes. Paw Paw’s Place is a ministry-owned nonprofit 501(c)(3). Your business helps support recovery, discipleship, and outreach in our community.
